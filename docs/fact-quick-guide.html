🚀 Quick Start

Three steps from a paper to a source-anchored appraisal.

Step 1

Sign in

Use Sign In at the top right. New accounts are approved by an administrator and unlock automatically.

Step 2

Give it a paper

In Analyze, upload a PDF, or paste a PubMed link, PMID, PMC ID or DOI.

Step 3

Read & click

Read the appraisal, then click any citation pill, such as [Res, p6], to jump to the exact source passage.

🔍 Find: Verified Literature Search

Describe a study in your own words, even a partial recollection. Searches PubMed & Europe PMC, and every result is verified against the published abstract.

How

Type, press Find

  • Natural language works: “CoQ10 and cardiovascular mortality, Swedish cohort”
  • Tick Bypass 24h Cache for a fresh search
Each result shows

Match, source, full-text status

  • % Match to your description
  • Source badge and Free Full Text status
  • Grounded Clinical Findings summary
Then

Analyze, add, or look for copies

  • Analyze Full Text opens it in Analyze
  • ➕ Add to Contextualizer collects it
  • 🎓 Scholar Versions ↗ looks for other free copies

🔬 Analyze: Structured, Source-Anchored Appraisal

F.A.C.T. identifies the study design, applies the methodological rules for that design, and anchors every claim to its source.

Input

Two ways in

  • 📄 PDF → Analyze
  • 🔗 Link / PMID (URL, PMID, PMC ID, DOI) → Fetch & Analyze
Output

Sections you get

  • Clinical Bottom Line
  • Results in Context (effects, CIs, NNT, harms)
  • Assertive Critical Appraisal
  • Objective, Design, Participants, Bibliographic Data
Verify

Click to check

  • Citation pills jump to the source passage
  • Hover the study-type badge to see which rules applied
  • Drag the divider to resize the panels

📄 Full Text vs. Abstract

F.A.C.T. always uses full text when it can get it, and it never analyzes an abstract without asking you first.

What you seeWhat it meansYour options
Free Full TextRetrieved automatically.Just press Fetch & Analyze.
Publisher Download RestrictionFree to read, but the publisher blocks automated download.Open the link, download the PDF, then 📤 Upload PDF Manually, or Analyze Abstract Only.
Full Text UnavailablePaywalled. Only the abstract is available.Analyze Abstract Only, or Cancel and get the paper first.
Abstract-only analyses are stamped [ABSTRACT-ONLY EVALUATION] on the source document, so they can't be mistaken for a full-text appraisal.
Tip, finding another free copy: click 🎓 Scholar Versions ↗ (or Find on Scholar in the dialog). The paper is normally the top result. Click All N versions under it, download the PDF, then use Upload PDF Manually.

❓ Interactive FAQ & Troubleshooting

Does F.A.C.T. ever analyze only an abstract without telling me?

No. Whenever only an abstract is available, whether from a paywall or a publisher block, F.A.C.T. stops and asks. Analysis proceeds only after you click Analyze Abstract Only.

The paper is free in my browser. Why won't F.A.C.T. fetch it?

Some publishers let people read a paper but block software from downloading it. F.A.C.T. tells you when this happens. Open the link it gives you, download the PDF yourself, then press 📤 Upload PDF Manually.

Why did I see “Access Authorization Pending”?

New accounts are reviewed by an administrator. The screen unlocks automatically the moment you are approved, with no refresh needed.

What is the difference between Analyze and Contextualize?

Analyze is a deep critical appraisal of one paper (full text where available). Contextualize is a broad synthesis across 3–10 papers, based on their abstracts.

My search results look out of date.

Find caches results for 24 hours. Tick Bypass 24h Cache (Force Refresh) and search again.

Can I trust the appraisal?

Treat it as a structured aid to your own judgment. Click the citation pills to confirm any claim that matters to a decision against the original passage.

🩺 Therapeutic Lens & Clinical Orientation

New in 2.0: Calibrate the clinical philosophy through which findings are synthesized.

Default

Integrative / Naturopathic

Prioritizes whole-person biology, nutritional biochemistry, and root-cause resilience. Actively suppresses reflexive conventional drug mandates (e.g. unsolicited statin therapy).

Consensus

Conventional Allopathic

Contextualizes findings within guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT) and prevailing standard-of-care disease algorithms.

Methodology

Pure Biostatistical

Maintains absolute clinical neutrality, focusing solely on power, confounding, selection bias, and endpoint dissociation.
